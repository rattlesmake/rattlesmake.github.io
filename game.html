THE GAME & THE ENGINE

An old passion.
A new foundation.

Centurion is our love letter to ancient-world strategy: a free-to-play RTS, built for online battles and the people who make a game their own.

CONCEPT ARTWORK

WHY WE’RE BUILDING IT

Keep the spirit.
Open new doors.

We know the pull of a great strategy game: learning an army, reading a battlefield, and coming back for one more match. Old RTS games gave us that feeling. Centurion is how we carry it forward.

Our goal is a place for players and creators alike. Online play brings people together; an editor and asset tools give their ideas a place to live.

UNDER THE STANDARD

Purpose-built for this world.

A custom engine gives us the freedom
to shape the game we believe in.

01

C++20

The engine’s foundation

The core is written in C++20, with the rattlesmake framework providing reusable systems for the world, assets, input, and pathfinding.

02

OpenGL / GLSL

A classic perspective, with depth

OpenGL and GLSL bring together three-dimensional terrain and two-dimensional sprites for Centurion’s distinctive 2.5D world.

03

XML assets

A game built from building blocks

Object definitions and visual assets are described in XML. Keeping content separate from engine code supports our ambition for a moddable game.

04

Creative tools

From an idea to the battlefield

The Editor and Assets Manager connect terrain, object data, and PNG-based sprite artwork in a workflow built around making things.

The wider toolkit includes GLFW, Dear ImGui, OpenAL, and TinyXML2. LuaJIT and sol2 provide infrastructure for future scripting work.
