A few things to know.

When will the alpha be available?

The date is still to be defined. Join our Discord community for release announcements and details about how to take part.

Does the alpha include the full Centurion vision?

No. The first alpha focuses on one civilization, four units, and online matches for up to four players. The wider vision includes at least eight civilizations, a moddable game, and creative tools. Explore the full project →

How can I be part of the community?

Join our Discord, introduce yourself, and tell us what draws you to strategy games. We welcome long-time fans of old RTS games, new players, and creators alike.
