MOVEMENT & FORMATIONS

An army. Not a crowd.

A good plan starts before the first clash. Pathfinding takes your orders across the battlefield, finding routes around the objects that stand in your way. Formations give those orders shape, turning a group of units into a force you can position with intent.

  • Navigate around buildings and obstacles.
  • Bring units together in organized formations.
  • Choose your approach before committing to a fight.

MELEE & RANGED COMBAT

Close the distance. Or keep it.

The meeting of melee and ranged units is at the heart of our vision for Centurion. Put troops where their role matters: send close-combat units into the clash and position ranged units to attack from a distance. The pleasure is in making an army work together.

  • Distinct roles for melee and ranged units.
  • Positioning that gives your decisions weight.
  • Direct control over the action.

TERRAIN, OBJECTS & STRONGHOLDS

Every battlefield has character.

An ancient world should be more than a backdrop. Terrain, buildings, and placed objects shape the spaces your army moves through. Strongholds give that world a center of gravity: a place to rally around, defend, and challenge.

  • A 2.5D world of terrain and sprite-based objects.
  • Distinctive Roman architecture.
  • Strongholds at the heart of the battlefield.
