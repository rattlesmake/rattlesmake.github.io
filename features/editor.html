Editor.

From the first brushstroke to your next great battlefield.

TERRAIN & BRUSHES

The landscape is your canvas.

Give a battlefield its shape and texture. Terrain brushes bring the practical tools of world building together: sculpt the land, paint its surface, and work from broad shapes down to the details that make a place memorable.

  • Paint terrain textures with brushes.
  • Shape heights and refine the landscape.
  • Build the setting around your idea.

OBJECT PLACEMENT

Turn a landscape into a place.

A stretch of land becomes a world when you start placing things in it. Bring buildings, units, and decorations into your map. Compose a stronghold, frame an approach with trees, or set the scene for an encounter.

  • Place game objects directly into the world.
  • Arrange buildings and decorations together.
  • See how your scene takes shape.

ADVENTURES & RANDOM MAPS

A starting point for your imagination.

Centurion’s creative vision brings authored adventures and scenarios together with generated battlefields. Build around an idea of your own, or use random map generation as the starting point for a new setting. The goal is a game with room for the community’s imagination.

  • Create and organize adventures and scenarios.
  • Explore battlefields through random map generation.
  • Make space for your own stories and encounters.
